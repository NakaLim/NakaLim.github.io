Stir Fried Ginger w/t Chicken

Ingredients

  • 1/2 bone in chicken breast.
  • 1 whole chicken leg.
  • 6 garlic roughly chopped.
  • 3 stalks green onion.
  • 1 lbs fresh ginger ( julienne).

Instructions

  1. Prep the ingredients: Slice chicken breast, garlic, green onions, and julienne fresh ginger.
  2. Cook chicken: Heat oil in a pan, cook chicken until browned, then set aside.
  3. Stir-fry ginger & garlic: In the same pan, stir-fry garlic and ginger until fragrant.
  4. Combine: Add cooked chicken and green onions, season with soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar.
  5. Serve: Stir everything together and serve hot with rice.
